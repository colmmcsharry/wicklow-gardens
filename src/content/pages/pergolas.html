Custom Pergola Installation in Dublin, Wicklow, Kildare & Louth

Transform your garden with a custom-built pergola that creates the perfect outdoor living space. We install timber and aluminium pergolas throughout Dublin, Wicklow, Kildare, and Louth, offering free-standing, wall-mounted, and roofed options. Perfect for creating shade and defining outdoor areas, pergolas add both beauty and functionality to your garden.

Our pergola installations are designed to complement your existing garden and home architecture. Whether you're looking for a traditional timber pergola for a Wicklow country garden or a modern aluminium structure for a Dublin city patio, we can create the perfect solution. Timber pergolas offer a natural look while aluminium provides a low-maintenance, modern alternative.

From simple structures that provide partial shade to fully roofed pergolas that create all-weather outdoor rooms, we offer solutions for every garden size and budget. Contact us today to discuss your pergola project and start creating your perfect outdoor space.
