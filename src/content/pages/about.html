About Wicklow Gardening and Landscaping

Wicklow Gardening and Landscaping is a family-owned business with over 10 years of experience transforming outdoor spaces across Dublin, Wicklow, Kildare, and Louth. We specialize in premium driveway installation, gardening, patio construction, and complete landscape design.

Our commitment to quality craftsmanship, attention to detail, and customer satisfaction sets us apart. Every project is completed to the highest standards using only the finest materials, and we guarantee our workmanship. Your satisfaction is our priority, and we stand behind every job we complete.

We take pride in every project we complete. As a family business, our reputation is built on trust, quality, and ensuring our customers are completely satisfied with the results. Michael Founder & Owner
