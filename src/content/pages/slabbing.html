Professional Slab Installation in Dublin, Wicklow, Kildare & Louth

Professional garden slab installation for paths, patios, and walkways across Dublin, Wicklow, Kildare, and Louth. Our expertly laid slabs create durable, attractive surfaces that enhance your garden while providing practical pathways and outdoor areas. We pay attention to every detail, ensuring your slabbing is level, properly spaced, and built to last.

Garden slabbing offers versatility and style, with numerous sizes, colors, and finishes available. We work with various slab materials including natural stone, concrete, and porcelain. Natural stone slabs provide a traditional Irish look, while modern concrete and porcelain options offer contemporary style and low maintenance.

Proper installation ensures your slabbing remains stable and level for years to come. We ensure correct base preparation, proper spacing, and expert jointing to create a professional finish. Contact us today for a free quote on your garden slabbing project.
