Our Services

Professional Landscaping & Gardening Services

From regular maintenance to full garden transformations, we offer a practical range of gardening and landscaping services across Dublin, Wicklow, Kildare, and Louth.


Click a service below to learn more.

  • Landscaping

    Complete garden design and transformation — beds, lawns, features, and outdoor spaces tailored to your home.

  • Garden Maintenance

    Regular or one-off visits for weeding, pruning, tidying, and seasonal care that keeps gardens looking their best.

  • Lawn Care

    Mowing, feeding, moss control, and scarifying for healthier, greener lawns throughout the year.

  • Hedge Trimming

    Neat hedge cutting and shaping with cuttings cleared away — tidy boundaries and healthier growth.

  • Planting & Borders

    Beds and borders designed for Irish conditions — colour, structure, and planting that lasts.

  • Turf Laying

    Supply and lay quality turf for an instant green lawn, with proper ground prep and aftercare advice.

  • Garden Clearance

    Clear overgrown gardens and debris so you have a clean slate for planting or landscaping.

  • Patios

    Outdoor living spaces designed to complement your home — practical, durable, and built for Irish weather.

  • Pergolas

    Custom pergolas for shade and structure — a simple way to upgrade outdoor seating areas.

  • Fencing

    Privacy and boundary fencing installed with quality materials and a clean professional finish.

  • Garden Walls

    Garden walls and pillars for structure, raised beds, and defined outdoor spaces.

  • Something Else?

    Need a different garden job? Get in touch and we will help where we can.
